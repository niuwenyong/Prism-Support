Prism Privacy Policy Effective date: November 6, 2026 We do not create user accounts, upload device information or usage records to developer servers, or integrate advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. 1. Device and monitoring data The app reads device model, system version, memory, CPU, network traffic, volume storage capacity and battery readings made available by the system while the app is in the foreground. Monitoring charts and session summaries are held in memory and are not retained after clearing them or quitting the app. Network interface addresses appear only on the local device page. System reports exclude those addresses, device name, serial number and IMEI. 2. Content you choose Text, QR codes, image recognition, scanning and PDF processing run on the device. The photo picker supplies only the images you select; the system may download cloud photos from iCloud. Contact selection uses only the chosen contact's name, organization, phone numbers and email addresses. Photos, addresses and notes are not saved, and the entire address book is not read. 3. System actions and permissions The camera is used only when you start recognition or scanning. Notification permission is used for local timed reminders; the app does not register for remote push notifications. Calendar, dialing, messages, mail, printing, sharing and Wi-Fi configuration are handed off through system interfaces or public APIs. Saving, sending, printing or joining a network may require your confirmation in a system interface. Wi-Fi passwords are not written to Prism's persistent storage. Network configurations saved by the system are managed by the system. 4. Local storage and deletion Sampling settings, haptic preferences and shortcut names are saved in local preferences. Generated and imported files are held in the app cache and excluded from backup. Old cached files older than 24 hours are cleaned up when subsequent files are generated. Copies you save to Files, Photos or Calendar, or send to others, are managed by their destinations and are not deleted with the app cache. You can revoke camera and notification permissions in Settings, turn off sampling, cancel local reminders, or uninstall the app to delete its local data. 5. Sharing and external links The app does not automatically upload content. When you use system sharing, communication or external links, recipients, chosen services and Apple system features process data under their own policies. Provide only necessary information in feedback. Avoid sending private photos, contacts, network configurations or full device logs. 6. Children and policy updates The product is not designed specifically for children. The developer does not collect personal data. This policy will be updated when features or processing practices change. 7.
