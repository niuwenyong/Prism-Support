Prism

Device information and local tools. Monitor your system, create QR codes, recognize text and work with PDFs.

Help and feedback

Contact the developer through GitHub Issues. Include your device model, OS version, steps and expected result. Issues are public: do not post passwords, contacts, private images, serial numbers or complete device logs.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the battery percentage differ?
iOS may round values from its public battery API. Matching the status bar at 1% granularity is not guaranteed.

Can Prism show a temperature in degrees?
iOS exposes a thermal state, not the phone’s internal temperature in degrees.

Why does available storage differ from Settings?
The estimate may include reclaimable space. Volume capacity, accounting methods and update times may differ.

Why is a feature unavailable?
Flashlight, scanning, Wi-Fi configuration, SMS, email and audio routes depend on hardware, permissions and system setup. Prism does not control the system-wide Wi-Fi or Bluetooth switches.

How do I remove data?
Clear the monitoring session, cancel reminders or uninstall the app. Copies you saved or shared to other destinations must be deleted there.
